About Couple Compatibility Test

Free couple compatibility test — answer 10 fun questions and get a detailed compatibility report with scores across 5 dimensions. All processing happens locally in your browser — no signup, no upload, no data collection.

How It Works

The test asks 10 questions about your relationship dynamics. Each answer contributes to scores across 5 dimensions: personality, values, lifestyle, communication, and intimacy. Results are combined with a name-based algorithm for a fun compatibility score.

5 Compatibility Dimensions

  • Personality Match — temperament and social style alignment
  • Shared Values — alignment on life priorities
  • Lifestyle Fit — daily habits and spending compatibility
  • Communication — conflict resolution and emotional understanding
  • Intimacy & Romance — affection and emotional connection
