Free Couple Compatibility Test

Discover how compatible you and your partner are with our free couple compatibility test. Answer 10 fun questions about your relationship dynamics, lifestyle preferences, and communication style. Get an instant, detailed compatibility report with scores across 5 key dimensions. No signup, no download — everything runs in your browser.

5 Compatibility Dimensions

  • Personality Match — How well your temperaments and social styles complement each other
  • Shared Values — Alignment on what matters most in life and relationships
  • Lifestyle Fit — Compatibility in daily habits, spending, and living preferences
  • Communication — How well you handle conflict and express feelings
  • Intimacy & Romance — Emotional and physical connection alignment

How It Works

Enter both names, then answer 10 multiple-choice questions about your relationship. Each answer contributes to scores across the 5 dimensions. The results are combined with a name-based compatibility algorithm for a fun overall score. The same names always produce the same base score, but your answers shape the detailed breakdown.

Fun Ways to Use It
